What a good quote includes

  • Your business name and how to contact you.
  • The customer's name and the job address.
  • A plain description of the work, so there's no argument later about what was included.
  • Materials and labour, and whether VAT is included.
  • How long the price stands, because material prices change.

Quote or estimate?

A quote is a fixed price the customer can accept. An estimate is a best guess that can change. If you're giving a fixed price, call it a quote and say what would change it, for example finding rotten joists once the floor is up.

Materials markup

Most trades add 10–20% to materials to cover collecting them, waste, returns and the risk of price rises. Show the marked-up price, not your trade price.

Questions

Is this quote generator free?

Yes, completely free with no signup. Your details are only saved in your own browser so they're filled in next time.

How do I save the quote as a PDF?

Press Print or save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer. On a phone, use Share then Print, then pinch out to save it as a PDF.

Should I add VAT to my quote?

Only if you're VAT registered. Below the £90,000 turnover threshold most sole traders aren't, so they don't charge VAT.
