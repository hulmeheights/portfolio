Why a direct review link works

Asking "could you leave us a Google review?" makes the customer search for you, find the listing and hunt for the button. Most give up. A direct link opens the star rating box straight away, so it takes them about 20 seconds.

When to send it

  • Right after the job, while they're happy: by text, not email.
  • On your invoice and receipt.
  • As a QR code on the van, business cards and the counter.

Message you can copy

"Thanks again for having us. If you were happy with the work, a quick Google review really helps a small local business: [your link]. Takes 20 seconds."

Don't offer discounts or gifts for reviews. Google's rules don't allow paid or incentivised reviews.

Questions

How do I get a direct link to my Google reviews?

Find your Place ID with Google's Place ID finder, paste it here and the tool builds the link that opens the review box. If Google Business Profile already gave you a share link, paste that instead to get the QR code and card.

Is it free?

Yes. No signup, and nothing is stored on our servers. Your details stay in your browser.

Can I give customers a discount for a review?

No. Google doesn't allow incentivised reviews, and it can remove them. Just ask at the right moment and make it one tap.
